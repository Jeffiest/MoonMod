---
name: reflect
description: End-of-session review. Use when a session is wrapping up, or when the user says reflect, to turn what went wrong into a small proposed edit.
---

1. List what went wrong or took too long this session, with the evidence (the failed check, the repeated question, the wasted step).
2. For each item, propose the smallest edit to a skill or to CLAUDE.md that would have prevented it.
3. Show the edits and wait for the user's OK. Change nothing without it.
