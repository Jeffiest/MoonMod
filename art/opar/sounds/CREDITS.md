# Opar's voice: sources

All CC0 (public domain), by EZduzziteh on OpenGameArt, downloaded 2026-10-30 (`src/`, the files as published):

- "Frog Croaks", https://opengameart.org/content/frog-croaks: croak_01.mp3 to croak_04.mp3
- "Ribbit Frog Sounds", https://opengameart.org/content/ribbit-frog-sounds: ribbit_01.mp3 to ribbit_03.mp3

The `.wav` beside them are mono 44.1 kHz conversions (ffmpeg). `art/opar/make_voice.py` builds his voice bank from them.
No attribution is required under CC0; this file records where they came from.
