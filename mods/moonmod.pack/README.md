# MoonMod Pack

The MoonMod mods in one mod. Turn the pack on in the Mod Manager and every part comes on with it; each part
is listed under the pack with its own switch and settings, and keeps its own saved progress (the same as when
it was a mod of its own, so nothing has to move).

Generated by `tools/pack/build_pack.py` from the parts' own folders in `mods/`. Each part's own notes are in
`docs/<part id>/`.

| Part | Version | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Autosave (`moonmod.autosave`) | 1.0.0 | Controls Chronicle's guarded native town autosave slot. |
| Controls (`moonmod.controls`) | 1.0.0 | Dungeon quick-item hotkeys, party shortcuts, and first-person toggle using MoonFactory input actions. |
| Expanded Town Camera (`moonmod.expanded-town`) | 1.0.0 | Toggle the legacy overhead camera while walking in towns. |
| Modern Cloud (`moonmod.modern-cloud`) | 1.0.1 | Quality of life for the story game, each part its own setting: thirst down to none, slower weapon wear, a weapon guard that warns, repairs from your Repair Powder or never lets a weapon break, the floor map and monsters shown, quicker character switches, a quicker walk, and keys 1, 2 and 3 for the quick item slots. |
| SirFancyBacon Mod Pack (`moonmod.sirfancybacon`) | 0.1.0 | Quality-of-life ideas from SirFancyBacon's Chronicle branches (qol-pack, floor-select), as a MoonMod mod. |
| Living Towns (`moonmod.living-towns`) | 1.0.0 | The towns ask things of you after their houses are rebuilt. |
| Dungeon+ (`moonmod.dungeon-plus`) | 2.1.1 | Champions with themed loot. |
| RPG: Character Paths (`moonmod.rpg`) | 2.0.1 | A real character build for every hero. |
| Dark Genie's Gauntlet (Roguelike) (`moonmod.roguelike`) | 0.3.0 | A roguelike game mode on the title menu. |
| Opar in the Story (`moonmod.opar`) | 0.1.0 | Opar, the frog mage, joins the story with Xiao. |
| Vigil (`moonmod.vigil`) | 0.1.1 | Souls-lite combat for the story game, first prototype (S1). |
| MoonMod Hub (`moonmod.hub`) | 1.1.0 | Quick links to the integrated progression, dungeon, controls, autosave and Gauntlet pages in the MoonFactory Mod Manager. |

Needs a MoonFactory engine that knows merged packs (`parts` in mod.json); older engines list the pack as
invalid and leave it off.
